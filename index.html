01

The North Star

Get the work done without losing respect or trust. Stay real, stay straight, and still be a cushion for the team.

I follow the North Star. And the deadline.

What I will not lose

Trust. Respect. People being real. No snitching. No fake team. When people can be themselves, they work better and they are happier. When I have to make a hard call, the order is work first, people second, my own peace last.

Get the job done. Don’t get the people done.

How this gets tested

Group discussions. Saying no. Using AI. I can use AI to save time. I will not let it do the thinking for me. If a group starts getting political or fake, I want to be the person who keeps it honest and still gets the job done.

How I want to be described

Kartik is the person who gets the job done no matter what. Straightforward, but still a cushion for the team.

Part airbag. Still hitting the gas.

At graduation I want to be ready for anything. I have done a lot in life, and this MBA is me investing in myself. For me, the sky is the limit. I do not expect to break every barrier by the end of the program. I want the mindset and the energy to face whatever comes after.

02

The Digital Contract

When I am offline

I am basically never offline. You will usually find me working on something. I know that can look like a bad habit. For me it is not. I like it. It keeps my productivity up.

The right to disconnect? I keep leaving that invite on read.

My real problem is not my phone. It is my own head. I overthink. My mind is the enemy. Music is how I handle that. It keeps me on tempo so I do not sit there stuck in my thoughts.

AI protocol

I will use AI to improve structure and help with research. That saves me time.

I will not use AI to do the work for me.

AI is my tool, not my replacement.

AI can cook. I still taste-test.

Where I show up

@schoolofhardknocks

Instagram. The one channel that actually keeps my target clear. That is where I want to be pointed.

For the MBA world

LinkedIn

I am not living there yet. I will use it for the program, internships, and connections. Same voice. Less noise.

LinkedIn is the group project of the internet. I will still show up.

The persona

Bet on yourself. Stay real.

Not fake corporate talk. Not pretending I have it all figured out. Just someone who does the work and does not fold.

03

The Resilient Mindset

I am not at mastery on anything, and I am not going to fake that. Most of this is still developing. Naming the gap is the point.

Critical thinking

Developing · closer to Beginning

I can think things through when I have to. The gap is confidence. Sometimes I know the point and I still do not trust myself enough to hold it.

I think critically. I just don’t always believe me.

Global community citizen

Developing

I can work with people. I try to respect them and be a cushion. The gap is bluntness. People who take things more personally can read that as cold.

Growth mindset

Developing

This MBA is me betting on myself. I do not think I am finished. The gap is overthinking. When I start doubting, I can get stuck in my head instead of staying in the work.

Professional values

Developing

Respect, trust, loyalty, and staying real already matter to me. The gap is when I lock onto the work. I can overlook the personal side.

Technical / digital literacy

Advancing

Tools and AI are comfortable. I already have a rule for them. The gap is using LinkedIn on purpose, not only the pages that keep me hungry.

The signal

Overthinking ramps up hard

That is how I know I am stressed. Not the inbox. Not the calendar. My head gets loud.

If overthinking burned calories, I would be shredded. FIFA is Plan B.

  • Dance in my room alone

The life-raft

  • My closest friend — the one who has seen me struggling
  • A walk, if he is not around
  • Family

04

The Inclusive Leader

01

What I already bring

Respect when the job is done. Trying to be a cushion. Care about trust and people being real. I will do that on purpose in every group project, not only when the team is already easy.

02

Where I will struggle

People take my directness the wrong way. I can be very blunt. Sometimes I overlook personal relations because I am locked on the work. Same honesty. One extra check: is this about the work, or am I running over the person?

I don’t sugarcoat. I straight-shot, then I pass the water.

03

The safety I want

People should be able to do what they think is correct. I can be wrong. I am not perfect. I will say that out loud early, especially when I am the one pushing. If someone disagrees, they should still feel like they can say it.

What will be in my briefcase

Hands

Be a real leader. Be an asset. Understand things I do not even know exist today.

Head

A mindset that keeps wanting more, keeps going forward, and flies high.

Heart

Respect. Realness. Loyalty. Hard work. Emotional awareness for the people on my team.

How I build it: team projects. Talking to people in my batch, not only the ones I already like. The internship. Making connections. Showing up where I am required and getting the job done.
